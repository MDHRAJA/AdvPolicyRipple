# Ethics & Limitations

- AI agents are simulated entities, not humans.
- Synthetic populations are not representative demographic evidence.
- Model assumptions influence results.
- LLM outputs, when enabled, are not automatically human behavior.
- Observed evidence must remain distinct from simulation output.
- The tool is decision support, not autonomous policymaking.
- Do not use sensitive demographic characteristics to make deterministic behavioral claims.
- Results should be validated against empirical evidence before real-world decisions.
