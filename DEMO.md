# 3-minute demo

**Live app:** [https://policy-forge-nu.vercel.app/](https://policy-forge-nu.vercel.app/)

**Pitch:** “Before deploying a policy on millions of people, what if we could test it on a digital society first?”

1. Open the landing page.
2. Use Water Rationing at 25%.
3. Run 500 agents, 20 rounds, seed 42.
4. Point out resource, stress and trust trajectories.
5. Change rationing to 40% while keeping population and seed constant.
6. Compare final metrics.
7. Explain unintended consequences as model-derived signals.
8. Use the assessment and calibration API for multi-seed uncertainty and evidence-aware learning.
9. Finish with limitations: synthetic data, assumptions, uncertainty, and no claim of human prediction.
